OffSeq Mirage is a honeypot threat-intelligence network run by OffSeq. Its sensors emulate exposed services and record the hosts that attack them.

This feed contains the IPv4 addresses that attacked the Mirage honeypot fleet in the last 7 days. An IP is listed only after it completes a handshake with a sensor, so spoofed sources are excluded, and known research scanners are kept out of this list.

The feed is rebuilt daily and published under CC BY 4.0 and TLP:CLEAR. Documentation and other formats are at github.com/offseq/mirage-feed.
